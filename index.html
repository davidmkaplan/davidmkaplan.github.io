David M. Kaplan

I am an Associate Professor in the School of Psychological Sciences at Macquarie University, where I serve as Head of Neuroscience Education.

My research sits at the intersection of motor neuroscience, systems neuroscience, and the philosophy of neuroscience. I study the computational processes underlying movement planning and sensorimotor learning, including how sensory uncertainty shapes learning and how expert motor skills develop in minimally invasive and robotic surgery.

Research themes

Sensorimotor learning

How people plan, control, and adapt movement when sensory information is uncertain.

Expert performance

The sensorimotor challenges of minimally invasive and robotic surgery.

Philosophy of neuroscience

Explanation, modelling, measurement, neural computation, and model organisms.

Background

I completed a PhD in Philosophy and Cognitive Neuroscience at Duke University in 2007. I then held postdoctoral fellowships at Washington University in St. Louis before joining Macquarie University.
